The Festival Culture

Built on Competition.
Proven by Character.

Every year, the Dar-e-Arqam Sports Festival turns our playing fields into something closer to a stadium — flags flying, teams marching in, and a whole region watching to see who takes the title.

It starts close to home. The Regional Sports Festival brings every campus across the Gujrat Region onto one ground, competing across nine disciplines for a single trophy and a year of bragging rights. The campus that wins doesn't just take a title — it earns the right to represent Gujrat at the next level.

That next level is the National Sports Festival, where our region's best athletes compete against every region of Pakistan under one banner. It is where discipline built on a school ground meets the scale of a national tournament — and where, in 2023, Gujrat Region brought a championship home.

Spirit of CompetitionEvery match is played to win — but always played fair, campus against campus, region against region.
TeamworkNo single athlete carries a campus. Every trophy is the sum of a whole team pulling in the same direction.
DisciplineMonths of training on the campus ground come down to a single festival day — and it shows.
Regional to NationalThe journey doesn't end with a campus trophy — it's the first step toward representing Gujrat at the National Sports Festival.
REGIONAL
Gujrat Region

Regional Sports Festival Gujrat Region

Every campus across the Gujrat Region — from Abubakar to Mukabbir, Fatima to Mirpur — sends its strongest athletes to one ground for the Regional Sports Festival. Days of finals across nine disciplines build to a single moment: the trophy raised by that year's winning campus, held for the year until the next festival begins.

2024 2023 2022 2021
2024

Mukabbir Campus Holds the Title

The 2024 festival opened with a decorated stage and an address from the chief guest, before campuses marched in behind their flags. Mukabbir Campus raised the trophy at the close — their title to defend next year.

Mukabbir Campus
2023

An Aerial View of the Whole Region

2023's opening ceremony filled the entire ground with formation displays, captured from above. Mukabbir Campus went back-to-back, and one athlete stood out enough to be named Sportsman of the Year.

Mukabbir Campus
2022

Mukabbir's First Title Under the Lights

2022 saw Mukabbir Campus lift the trophy for the first time captured here, celebrated with a full team photograph beneath the festival backdrop before prizes were handed out match by match.

Mukabbir Campus
2021

Torches, and a Festival Under the Night Sky

The earliest festival captured here opened after dark, with a mounted torch-bearer and a hand-to-hand flame relay between guests of honour — a dramatic start to a weekend of competition that Mukabbir Campus's "Stallions" would go on to be part of.

National Sports Festival All Pakistan

Beyond the region, the National Sports Festival gathers every region of Pakistan under one banner. Teams take the field under franchise-style names, competitions run across two full days, and the festival closes the way every great tournament does — with an opening ceremony worth remembering and a champion worth celebrating.
