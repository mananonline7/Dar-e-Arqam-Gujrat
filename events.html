Event One

Annual Prize Distribution

“Success in this world and the world hereafter.”

Dar-e-Arqam Schools, Gujrat Region

Once a year, an entire school year's worth of effort is called out loud — name by name, campus by campus — and the Annual Prize Distribution becomes the proudest evening on our calendar.

It is the evening where quiet, patient hard work finally steps into the light. Toppers and position holders are recognised in front of the very teachers and parents who watched them grow all year, and their achievement is read out for the whole gathering to applaud. But the ceremony is never only for the highest scorers — students recognised for discipline, character, sport, and steady improvement are named with the same warmth and the same applause.

Campus after campus across the Gujrat Region brings its own achievers to this stage, and for one evening the entire region gathers under a single banner to say the same thing: excellence, once noticed, is excellence repeated. A trophy raised here rarely stays a private achievement for long — it becomes the reason a younger sibling, a classmate, or the student sitting one row back quietly decides to try a little harder next year.

Event Two

Annual Teachers Conference

Each year, Dar-e-Arqam Schools brings its entire teaching staff together under one roof for a day of training, recognition and collaboration — the kind of investment in our teachers that shows up in every classroom afterwards. Below are the 2025 and 2026 conferences.

2026

ATC 2026

The 2026 conference brought staff from across the Gujrat Region together for a day of workshops, certificates and shared purpose. Best Performance Award recipients were honoured on stage, and every teacher who completed the year's training left with a certificate in hand — a small, deliberate reminder that their effort in the classroom is seen and valued.

2025

ATC 2025

The theme of the 2025 conference — "From the Teacher's Guidance to Nation-Building" — set the tone for the day: keynote sessions on classroom leadership, certificates and shields presented to outstanding staff, and a room full of educators reminded that the work they do reaches far beyond their own classroom walls.

Muhabbat-e-Rasool Conference

سیرتِ رسول ﷺ سے محبت کا سالانہ اجتماع

An annual gathering of love for the Seerah of the Prophet ﷺ

Once a year, Dar-e-Arqam Schools, Gujrat Region, sets aside a single day for one purpose: to gather the whole school community — students, teachers, parents and guests — around the life and love of the Holy Prophet ﷺ.

The Muhabbat-e-Rasool Conference has grown into one of the largest gatherings on our calendar — a massive Seerat-un-Nabi ﷺ conference held under a tented pavilion large enough for every campus to attend together. Speech and debate competitions bring students to the podium to speak on the Seerah and its lessons, judged in front of the very community they represent.

The conference is also where the year's Huffaz — students who have completed the memorisation of the Holy Qur'an — are formally recognised, called to the stage one by one to receive their prizes and certificates in front of the whole gathering. The day closes with an address from the chief guest, whose words send everyone home with something to carry into the year ahead.
