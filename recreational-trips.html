Beyond the Classroom, Beyond the Border

Why We Travel

At Dar-e-Arqam Schools, Gujrat Region, we believe the world itself is a classroom. Our international recreational trips take students and staff beyond Pakistan's borders — to walk different streets, meet different people, and see how other nations teach, learn and live. Every journey returns home as a story, a friendship and a slightly wider view of what's possible.

The Purpose

More Than a Holiday

Every trip is planned around four simple goals.

Broadening Horizons

Stepping outside Gujrat, and outside Pakistan, shows students how much bigger the world is — and how capable they are of navigating it.

Cultural Exposure

From the multicultural streets of Kuala Lumpur to the heritage of Sri Lanka, students meet traditions and ways of life different from their own — and return home more empathetic for it.

Educational Benefits

Our administration studies international schooling models, campus design and classroom technology firsthand — insights that come home and shape how we teach.

Memorable Experiences

Landmarks, new friendships and shared adventure turn a school trip into a memory that outlasts the classroom it was meant to complement.

🇲🇾Tour De Malaysia · 2022

Malaysia

Five Days Across the Peninsula

Tour De Malaysia carried a delegation of Dar-e-Arqam students and staff from Gujrat to Kuala Lumpur and on to the island of Langkawi — five days of landmarks, learning and laughter that none of them are likely to forget. It was the school's first international recreational trip, and it set the standard for everything that has followed.

The journey opened in Kuala Lumpur, beneath the Petronas Twin Towers and the needle-like Menara KL, before climbing into the clouds at Genting Highlands and standing before the golden statue that guards Batu Caves. From there, the group flew on to Langkawi, where a cable car climbed through rainforest to the Sky Bridge, the islands were explored by boat, and evenings closed with dinner, conversation and the kind of stories that only get better with each retelling.

“Every landmark was a photograph. Every evening together was the real souvenir.”

Beyond the sightseeing, the trip was a lesson in independence, teamwork and representing Dar-e-Arqam Schools with pride — students and teachers navigating a foreign country side by side, and coming home with a friendship forged well outside the classroom.

🇱🇰Extending the Journey South

Sri Lanka

The Next Chapter of Dar-e-Arqam's International Trips

Following the success of Tour De Malaysia, Dar-e-Arqam Schools has opened the opportunity of an international trip to Sri Lanka — for students and administration alike. It's a chance to trade Gujrat's classrooms for a different island, a different culture, and a fresh set of ideas about what education can look like.

Sri Lanka offers a different flavour of the same purpose: ancient Buddhist heritage sites, hill-country tea estates and the busy streets of Colombo, alongside visits designed to let our administration observe how schools elsewhere teach, build and inspire. Students and staff will return home, once again, with a wider view of the world and fresh perspectives to bring back into every Dar-e-Arqam classroom.

“Two countries in, and the lesson is the same every time — the world is bigger, and worth seeing.”
