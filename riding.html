Our Story

Saddle Up for a Grand Adventure

The Mukabbir Riding Club began with a simple idea: some lessons are learned far better in a saddle than at a desk. When a child looks a horse in the eye, tightens the reins and asks it to walk on, they learn something no textbook teaches — how to be calm, patient and brave, all at once.

“A horse doesn't care about your marks. It only responds to your patience, your kindness and your courage.”

What started as weekend rides for our hostel students has grown into a full club at our Mukabbir Campus grounds — open to riders from Class 1 right through Class 10, with dedicated girls' batches and family days. Every session is built around three things: safety first, character always, and adventure guaranteed.

Our young riders come for the thrill of the gallop. They leave with straighter posture, steadier nerves, and the quiet confidence that comes from having handled something far bigger than themselves.

Salient Features

What Makes Our Club Special

Six good reasons to hang your hat with us.

Open for Class 1 to Class 10

Every student from Class 1 through Class 10 is welcome in the saddle — no experience needed. Riders are grouped by age and ability, so a first-timer in Class 1 is never rushed and a confident Class 10 rider is never held back. Each group gets guidance pitched exactly at their level.

Separate Sessions for Girls (Class 5–10)

Our girls from Class 5 to Class 10 have their own dedicated riding batches, held separately with a comfortable, private and fully supervised environment. The atmosphere is calm and encouraging, so every girl can find her feet at her own pace. It is a space built for confidence, not competition.

Special Packages for Families

Riding is a whole lot more fun when the family joins in. We offer special family packages with parent-and-child sessions, family riding days and relaxed weekend visits to the grounds. Parents and siblings are always welcome to watch, cheer and take plenty of photographs.

Three Sessions Per Week

Riders saddle up three times a week on a fixed, structured timetable — enough to build real skill without ever clashing with schoolwork. The regular rhythm means progress you can actually see, week after week. Batch timings are shared with parents at the start of every term.

Professional Trainers to Guide You

Our trainers are experienced horsemen who have spent years in the saddle and know how to teach children. They stay within arm's reach of every beginner, correcting posture, steadying nerves and calling out encouragement. Safety is their first instruction and their last word.

Trained & Friendly Horses for Children

Our horses are hand-picked for temperament, then schooled specifically to work with young riders. They are calm, patient and completely used to small hands and nervous first attempts. Every horse is well cared for, regularly checked and matched to a rider's size and skill.

Training & Safety

How a Session Runs

Every ride follows the same trusted routine — steady, supervised and built for beginners.

Warm-Up & Meeting the Horse

Riders start on the ground — stretching, then greeting their horse, learning to approach calmly, lead it and build trust before anyone mounts up.

Mounting & Posture

Trainers teach the safe way to mount, then set the basics: heels down, back straight, eyes up. Good posture is where every good rider begins.

Balance & Control

At walking pace, riders practise balance in the saddle, holding the reins correctly, steering through turns and — most importantly — halting with confidence.

Progressive Levels

As confidence grows, riders move up: from a led walk, to riding independently, to steering courses and finally a controlled trot. Nobody advances before they're ready.

Helmets Always Proper Riding Gear Constant Supervision Strict Safety Rules

Helmets are compulsory for every rider, every session — no exceptions. Horses are checked and tacked up by trainers before riders arrive, and a trainer stays beside every beginner from the moment they mount to the moment they dismount.

Classes & Structure

Three Trails to Ride

Our programme runs across three levels from Class 1 to Class 10 — each one a step further down the trail.

Level One

The Cubs

Class 1 – 4

First contact with horses. Little riders learn to approach and pet a horse, sit correctly in the saddle, hold the reins and take their first led walk — with a trainer at the horse's head the whole way.

Level Two

The Rangers

Class 5 – 7

Riding on their own. Rangers take the reins independently, learn to steer, change direction and halt smoothly, and start reading their horse's mood. This is where nervous beginners turn into confident riders.

Level Three

The Mavericks

Class 8 – 10

Advanced control and discipline. Mavericks refine their seat, ride courses with precision and progress to a controlled trot — plus a share of the responsibility for grooming and caring for their horse.

Class
5 – 10
Girls' Sessions

A Batch of Their Own

Our girls from Class 5 to Class 10 ride in their own dedicated batch, scheduled separately from the boys' sessions. The grounds are reserved, the atmosphere is private and comfortable, and the focus stays entirely on learning.

Many of our girls arrive having never stood beside a horse before. Within a few weeks they are mounting on their own, steering through turns and asking when the next session is. That change — from cautious to capable — is exactly what these sessions are for.

  • Girls-only batch with reserved grounds and full privacy
  • Supportive, unhurried coaching — progress at your own pace
  • Same trained horses, same safety standards, same adventure
  • Confidence that carries straight back into the classroom

Family Packages

Bring the Whole Outfit

Riding shouldn't stop at the school gate. Our family packages open the grounds to parents and siblings, so the whole family can share the saddle — or simply pull up a chair and watch.

  • Family riding days — the grounds open for parents and siblings to ride alongside our students
  • Parent-and-child sessions — learn the basics together, side by side in the arena
  • Photo days — proper portraits of your rider in the saddle, helmet and all
  • Picnic-style weekends — relaxed mornings at the grounds with refreshments and horses to meet

Ask at the Mukabbir Campus office for current package rates and the next family day on the calendar.
