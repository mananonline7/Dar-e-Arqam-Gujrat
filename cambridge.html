Campus Overview

A Centre for High-Standard Education

Cambridge & Mukabbir Campus brings the full Dar-e-Arqam journey together on a single site — from a child's very first day in Pre-School right through to their O-Level examinations. It is a campus designed around one idea: that a student should never have to change schools to keep growing.

Set near Airport Chowk in Gujrat, the campus pairs a purpose-built learning environment with the discipline and warmth that families expect from Dar-e-Arqam. Classrooms are bright and well-equipped, the grounds are spacious and secure, and every stage of the academic path is staffed by teachers who specialise in that age group.

  • Academic excellence — a curriculum that builds from early literacy to Cambridge O-Levels
  • Safe environment — a secure, supervised campus with controlled access
  • Modern facilities — bright classrooms, activity spaces and expansive sports grounds
  • Holistic development — academics, Islamic values, sports and the arts, together
Academic Path

Pre-School to O-Levels — One Continuous Journey

Four stages, each building deliberately on the one before it.

Stage 01

Pre-School

A warm, nurturing first step into school life. Play-based learning introduces early literacy and numeracy through stories, songs and hands-on activity — so children learn to love school before they ever learn to sit an exam.

Stage 02

Primary

The foundation years. Students build genuine strength in core subjects — English, Mathematics, Science and Urdu — while Islamic values and character-building run through every school day, not just the timetable.

Stage 03

Middle School

Where understanding deepens. The focus shifts from memorising to reasoning — concept mastery, structured study habits, presentation and problem-solving skills that prepare students for the demands of the O-Level years ahead.

Stage 04

O-Levels

The rigorous Cambridge curriculum, taught by subject specialists. Students receive structured examination preparation, regular assessment and one-to-one guidance on choosing subjects and mapping out their college and career pathways.

Facilities & Campus Life

Built Around How Children Actually Learn

Bright classrooms, open grounds and spaces where the whole school comes together.

Modern Learning Environment

Classrooms are bright, colourful and arranged for activity rather than rows of silent desks. Displays, group tables and hands-on materials keep younger learners engaged, while senior classrooms are set up for focused, exam-standard study.

Digital Resources

Teaching is supported by multimedia and audio-visual aids, with computer literacy built into the programme so students keep pace with the wider world. Digital tools are used to explain concepts, not to replace the teacher.

Safe & Secure Campus

The campus is enclosed, supervised and access-controlled, with staff on duty throughout the day and a clear pick-up and drop-off routine. Parents can be confident their child is accounted for from arrival to home time.

Events & Highlights

Cambridge Campus Events & Achievements

The days our students remember long after the term ends.

Annual Ceremony

Annual Prize Distribution Ceremony

Our biggest evening of the year, when the campus gathers to honour its high achievers. Students who excelled in academics, attendance and co-curricular activities are called to the stage to receive certificates and shields before an audience of parents, teachers and invited guests.

The ceremony is as much a performance as a prize-giving — classes present tableaux, skits and speeches they have rehearsed for weeks. For younger children it is often their first time on a stage; for our seniors in graduation gowns, it is a send-off.

Annual Gala

Annual Sports Gala

The campus grounds fill with colour as every house marches past in formation to open the day. Races, team events and drill displays run from morning through to the medal ceremony, with parents lining the field to cheer.

Participation is the point — from Pre-School children in fancy-dress relays to senior athletes chasing a podium finish, every class has an event to enter. Winners take home medals, and everyone leaves with a sharper sense of teamwork and fair play.

Holiday Programme

Summer Camp

When the term ends, the grounds stay busy. Our summer camp keeps students active through the break with coached football sessions, fitness drills and team games on the campus field.

Children are grouped by age and worked through proper warm-ups, ball skills and small-sided matches by our coaches. It is a relaxed, energetic few weeks — and many students arrive back in September noticeably fitter and more confident.

Why Cambridge Campus

More Than a Curriculum

Three commitments that shape every classroom on this campus.

Dedicated Faculty & Guidance

Our teachers are qualified subject specialists, supported by ongoing training through the Dar-e-Arqam Training & Development department. Class sizes are kept deliberately manageable so that every student is known by name — and no quiet child slips through unnoticed.

Character & Values

Cambridge academics and Islamic character are taught side by side, not in competition. The day opens with recitation of the Holy Qur'an, Islamic manners are expected in every corridor, and discipline and respect are treated as seriously as any examination result.

Future Readiness

Cambridge O-Level qualifications are recognised by universities across the UK, North America, Europe and Asia. Beyond the certificate, our students leave with the independent study habits, written fluency and confidence that college and professional life demand.
