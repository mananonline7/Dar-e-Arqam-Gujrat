The Society Behind Every Team

Dar-e-Arqam Center for Sports & Learning (DSL)

Every team on this page — football, cricket, badminton, volleyball, table tennis and basketball — is run under one umbrella: the Dar-e-Arqam Center for Sports and Learning (DSL). DSL is the society responsible for coaching, training schedules, kits, facilities and inter-school fixtures across every Dar-e-Arqam campus in the Gujrat Region.

From the football pitch to the badminton court, DSL brings the same standard of structured coaching and competitive spirit to every sport we offer — because a great athlete, like a great student, deserves proper training.

Badminton Speed & Precision

Badminton at Dar-e-Arqam is more than a pastime — it's a proper competitive team, coached through regular indoor sessions on footwork, serves and rally control. Our players regularly test their skills in inter-school matches, building focus, agility and sportsmanship.

Coaches & Training

Structured drills under expert coaches sharpen reflexes and match strategy, preparing students for real competition.

03

Cricket Bat, Ball & Team Spirit

Our cricket team practises on the school field with proper kits, nets and coaching — batting, bowling and fielding drills that build real match temperament. The team regularly takes on other schools in friendly matches, carrying the same discipline and teamwork instilled in every practice session.

Coaches & Training

Dedicated coaches guide net practice and match strategy, helping players grow in confidence, technique and team spirit.

04

Volleyball Energy on Every Point

Volleyball brings out some of the liveliest energy on campus — a proper school team trained in serving, setting and spiking, cheered on by classmates at every match. Inter-school volleyball fixtures give our students real competitive experience, all while building trust and teamwork on the court.

Coaches & Training

Coaches run focused sessions on court positioning and team communication — the two pillars of a strong volleyball side.

05

Table Tennis Fast Hands, Faster Focus

Table tennis rewards focus, quick reflexes and precision — qualities our coached players sharpen through regular indoor practice. Our table tennis team represents Dar-e-Arqam in inter-school competitions, carrying forward the same discipline and calm confidence built during training.

Coaches & Training

One-on-one coaching attention helps every player refine their serve, spin and match temperament.

06

Basketball Court Craft

Our basketball team trains courtside under proper coaching — dribbling, shooting and defensive drills that build both skill and stamina. The team regularly faces other schools in friendly matches, and every game reflects the discipline, confidence and teamwork built on the practice court.

Coaches & Training

Expert coaches structure every session around fitness, technique and game sense — shaping confident, team-first players.
