Beyond the Classroom

Where Students Learn Who They Can Become

At Dar-e-Arqam Schools Gujrat Region, we believe learning extends beyond the classroom. Our youth camps are designed to inspire students, strengthen their character, and help them build essential life skills through meaningful experience — teamwork, leadership and spiritual growth. Each camp offers a balanced environment where students learn, explore and grow, while creating memories that stay with them long after camp ends.

28–30 October 2022 · Mukabbir Campus, Gujrat

URAAN — Character Development Camp

URAAN was a three-day residential character development camp held exclusively for Dar-e-Arqam students, set in the peaceful surroundings of the Mukabbir Campus. Away from their everyday routine, participants spent three days strengthening their minds, bodies and souls through a program of engaging activities and guided learning experiences.

Designed as a complete personal development program, URAAN focused on building confidence, responsibility, teamwork, leadership and Islamic values — encouraging every student to step outside their comfort zone and discover what they were truly capable of. The camp was organized by the dedicated team of Dar-e-Arqam Youth Gujrat, and became a memorable, transformative experience for every student who took part.

9–12 July 2023 · Mukabbir Campus, Gujrat

Alchemist Camp

ALCHEMIST CAMP was a four-day youth development camp that brought students together for a journey of learning, self-discovery and personal growth — combining educational, spiritual, recreational and leadership-focused activities to help participants build confidence, resilience and essential life skills.

Across the four days, students took part in a wide range of sessions and activities, including:

  • Quran and Hadith learning sessions
  • Sports competitions and physical activities
  • Storytelling sessions and bonfire night
  • Character-building workshops
  • Information Technology (IT) workshops
  • Emotional wellbeing & personal development
  • Leadership and teamwork training
  • Self-confidence enhancement activities
  • Career guidance and counselling sessions

ALCHEMIST CAMP gave students an enriching environment in which to strengthen their faith, develop leadership qualities, build lasting friendships and gain experiences that continue to benefit them — in the classroom and beyond.
